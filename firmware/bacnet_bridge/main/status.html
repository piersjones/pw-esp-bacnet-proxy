The ESP32 has no internal supply-voltage sensor, so this isn't a live voltage reading - it's the reason for the most recent boot. A reboot cause of "Brownout" or "Power glitch" means the supply sagged below ~2.43V at some point; seeing that repeatedly points at the power adapter or wiring, not the firmware.
